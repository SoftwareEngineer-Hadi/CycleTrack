CycleTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

CycleTrack ("we", "our", or "the app") is a privacy-first period and ovulation tracking application. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • Your cycle logs are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device.
  • Cloud backup is optional and uses end-to-end encryption before upload.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete all data in-app; retention and deletion steps are below.

Information you provide

You may optionally log:

  • Menstrual flow, symptoms, mood, intimacy, libido
  • Basal body temperature (BBT), weight, cervical mucus, pill intake, notes
  • App lock PIN (stored as a one-way hash on device only)
  • Pregnancy mode settings (if enabled)

This data is used only to display your calendar, predictions, analytics, insights, and reminders within the app.

Local storage

CycleTrack stores your data in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher) on your device. We do not transmit this data unless you explicitly enable cloud backup and sign in.

Optional cloud backup

If you choose Backup & restore and sign in with Google, the app encrypts your data on your device using AES-256-GCM, then uploads an opaque ciphertext blob to our Supabase backend. The server cannot read your plaintext cycle data. Your Google account is used only to identify your backup row.

You can delete your cloud backup at any time from Settings (see How to delete your data below).

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In — only if you enable cloud backup (authentication).
  • Supabase — stores encrypted backup blobs when you use cloud backup.
  • Google Fonts — typography may be loaded from Google servers.

The app works fully offline without these services except font loading.

Notifications

If you grant permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device for period reminders, fertile window, ovulation, late period alerts, and pill reminders. Notification content is generated locally; we do not send push notifications from a server.

Data retention

CycleTrack is designed so that we do not retain readable personal health data on our servers. Retention depends on how you use the app:

  • Local data (default) — Your cycle logs, settings, and app lock information remain on your device until you delete them in the app, use Delete everything in Settings, or uninstall CycleTrack. We do not automatically expire or delete local data on a fixed schedule.
  • Optional cloud backup — If you sign in with Google and back up, an encrypted backup blob is stored in our Supabase database and linked to your account. It is kept until you delete it (see below) or delete your backup account data. We do not use cloud backups for analytics or advertising.
  • Data we do not store — We do not operate separate server logs of your plaintext cycle entries, symptoms, notes, or health measurements. Optional Google Sign-In is handled by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for how Google retains authentication data.

If you only use CycleTrack offline and never enable cloud backup, all of your data stays on your device and is not retained by us.

How to delete your data

You can delete your data yourself at any time. You do not need to contact us for in-app deletion.

Delete all local and cloud data (recommended)

  1. Open CycleTrack.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll to Delete everything and confirm.

This permanently removes your local encrypted database, app lock, scheduled notifications, and your encrypted cloud backup (if you were signed in).

Delete cloud backup only

  1. Open Settings → Backup & restore.
  2. Sign in with Google if you are not already signed in.
  3. Sign out after use, or use Delete everything in Settings to remove the cloud copy along with local data.

Cloud backup deletion removes your encrypted backup row from our Supabase server. Because backups are encrypted on your device, we cannot recover plaintext data after deletion.

Delete local data only

  • Use Delete everything in Settings (also removes cloud backup if signed in), or
  • Uninstall CycleTrack from your device — this removes the local encrypted database.

Data deletion requests (contact)

If you cannot access the app (for example, you lost your device but had cloud backup enabled with Google Sign-In), you may request deletion of your cloud backup by contacting us:

  • Email or message the developer via the GitHub repository: open an issue with the subject Data deletion request and the Google account email used for backup.

We will verify ownership of the backup account and delete the associated encrypted cloud backup within 30 days. Local data on a lost device cannot be deleted remotely unless you had enabled cloud backup; uninstalling from a new device does not affect data still on an old device.

Data export

You can export a copy of your logs at any time: open Settings → Export CSV. The file is saved to your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Children

CycleTrack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Medical disclaimer

CycleTrack provides statistical estimates based on your logged history. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or contraception counseling. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption for local storage and cloud backups. No method of storage is 100% secure; use app lock and keep your device secure.
