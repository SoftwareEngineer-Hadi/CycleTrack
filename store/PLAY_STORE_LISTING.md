## Long description (max 4000 characters)

```
CycleTrack — your private period & ovulation companion

Track your menstrual cycle with confidence. CycleTrack is built privacy-first: use it completely offline with no account, or optionally back up encrypted data to the cloud with Google sign-in.

WHY CYCLETRACK?

• Privacy by default — no sign-up required to start tracking
• Encrypted local storage — your logs stay protected on your device
• Optional encrypted cloud backup — restore on a new phone with the same Google account
• Beautiful, calm design — light & dark themes

TRACK YOUR CYCLE

• Log flow, symptoms, mood, BBT, weight, intimacy, cervical mucus & pills
• Calendar view with period, fertile window & ovulation markers
• Cycle ring dashboard — see your current phase at a glance
• Predictions for next period, fertile window & ovulation day

INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS

• BBT and weight charts over time
• Cycle length history & averages
• Phase-based tips and symptom patterns
• Pregnancy mode with gestational week & milestones

STAY ON SCHEDULE

• Local reminders for period, fertile days, ovulation & late period
• Custom pill reminders
• All notifications run on-device — no server push required

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL

• App lock with PIN & fingerprint / Face ID
• Export CSV for doctor visits
• One-tap delete — wipe local data and cloud backup
• Cloud backup encrypts on your device before upload; our servers never see plaintext cycle data

IMPORTANT

CycleTrack provides statistical estimates based on your logged history. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or contraception counseling. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Download CycleTrack and take control of your cycle — privately.
```
